# Aangan — song prompt for an AI music generator

Written 27 August 2026. Paste one of the blocks below into the music tool.
For Instagram, use a reel block. The full song is underneath it.

The whole song rests on what the name already means. **Aangan / अंगण is the
courtyard of a house** — the open patch where the family sits. A student leaving
home for Kolhapur is losing exactly that. The song is about finding it again.

Do not make it an advertisement. There is no product in the lyrics, no website,
no "book now". The brand appears once, as the word that means home. This is the
same discipline as the owner pitch: never oversell, never claim what is not
there.

---

## Reel blocks — short, hook first, loopable

Use these for Instagram. A reel is not a song: the hook has to land in the first
two seconds, before anyone decides to scroll, and the end has to run back into
the start cleanly so a loop does not feel like a stop.

### Reel A — 15 seconds, the main one

```
Style: Punchy Marathi folk-pop hook. Bright, young, celebratory. Made for a
15-second vertical video. 96 BPM, 6/8 clap groove.

Instruments: dholki and hand claps up front and loud, fingerpicked acoustic
guitar underneath, one bansuri phrase as the tail. No intro, no build — the
first beat is at 0:00.

Vocals: One young male voice, close and warm, half-shouted on the hook like a
group of friends. Small group of voices answering him.

Loop: the last beat must run straight back into the first. No fade out.
Leave a half-second instrumental gap after each line for on-screen text.

[0:00 - cold open, claps, full voice]
अंगण! अंगण!

[0:03 - guitar and dholki, single voice]
गाव सुटलं, शहर नवं

[0:06 - answering voices]
रूम मिळाली, घर झालं

[0:09 - hook, everyone, claps loudest]
अंगण! अंगण!
कोल्हापुरात माझं अंगण!

[0:14 - one bansuri note, straight back into the top]
```

### Reel C — the chant, for students to use on their own videos

```
Style: Almost no melody. A clap-and-chant hook, the kind a group shouts.
Marathi folk. 100 BPM. 12 seconds. Loops forever.

Instruments: hand claps, dholki, one low guitar note holding underneath.
Nothing else. Raw, recorded like a hostel corridor, voices slightly off-mic.

Vocals: call and response. One voice calls, six voices answer.

[Call] अंगण?
[Answer] अंगण!
[Call] कुठे?
[Answer] कोल्हापुरात!
[All, claps]
घरापासून दूर, घरासारखं — अंगण!

Repeat once. End on a clap, not a fade.
```

### How to use them

- **Reel A** under listing photos, room walk-throughs, before-and-after cleanups.
- **Reel B** under a student's story — arriving, moving in, first day at college.
- **Reel C** under anything fast and funny, and it is the one students might
  reuse on their own videos. That is worth more than any advertisement you make.
- Put the brand name on screen, not in the lyrics. The song says **अंगण** for
  its meaning; the caption says what Aangan does.

## Block 1 — the full song

```
Style: Warm Marathi-Hindi acoustic folk-pop. Nostalgic but hopeful, never sad.
A young person's coming-of-age song, not a jingle.

Tempo: 88 BPM, gentle 6/8 sway.
Key: D major, with the verses leaning briefly minor.

Instruments: fingerpicked acoustic guitar as the spine; dholki (Maharashtrian
folk drum) entering at the first chorus; harmonium pads underneath; bansuri
(bamboo flute) answering the vocal line between phrases; light hand claps and a
small group of background voices on the final chorus. No EDM drop, no heavy
bass, no autotune, no electric guitar.

Vocals: One warm young male lead, chest voice, slightly imperfect and human,
like a hostel-room recording. A soft female harmony joins on the last chorus
only.

Mood arc: lonely bus window -> curiosity -> belonging -> warmth.
Production: intimate and dry. Guitar close-mic'd. Room reverb, not hall reverb.

Structure and lyrics:

[Intro - guitar and bansuri only, 8 bars]

[Verse 1 - quiet, guitar only]
गाव सुटलं, गाडी निघाली
आईचा डबा, बाबांचा हात
खिडकीच्या मागे सरतं शिवार
नवं शहर, नवी पहाट

[Pre-chorus - build, dholki enters softly]
कुठे राहू, कोण आपलं
अनोळखी रस्त्यावर मी एकटा

[Chorus - full, warm, singable]
अंगण... अंगण...
घरापासून दूर, तरी घरासारखं
अंगण... अंगण...
कोल्हापुरात मला माझं अंगण मिळालं

[Verse 2 - dholki steady now]
रंकाळ्यावर उतरली संध्याकाळ
महालक्ष्मीची दुरून घंटा
कॉलेज, अभ्यास, मेसचं ताट
नवे मित्र, नवं नातं

[Pre-chorus]
आता रस्ता ओळखीचा
आणि दार उघडं वाट बघतं

[Chorus - repeat, fuller]

[Bridge - drop to almost nothing, voice and harmonium]
काकूंचा आवाज — "जेवलास का रे?"
इतकंच पुरे, घर आठवायला
हजार किलोमीटर दूर आई हसते
फोनवर म्हणते — "बरं वाटलं"

[Final chorus - female harmony, claps, group voices]
अंगण... अंगण...
जिथे आपलं माणूस असतं, तेच घर
अंगण... अंगण...
कोल्हापुरात मला माझं अंगण मिळालं

[Outro - bansuri fades, single guitar note]
अंगण.
```

## Things to tell the tool if the first take is wrong

- Too fast or too "filmi": say **slower, 80 BPM, more intimate, fewer
  instruments**.
- Sounds like an advertisement: say **remove all brightness and polish, make it
  a folk song, one voice and one guitar**.
- Drums too heavy: say **dholki only, played with hands, no kick drum**.
- Voice too polished: say **untrained young voice, slight breath, recorded in a
  small room**.
- Wrong region: say **Maharashtrian folk, not Punjabi, not Bollywood**.

## What to check before using it anywhere

- Have a Kolhapur Marathi speaker read the lyrics aloud. Written Marathi and
  spoken Kolhapur Marathi are not the same thing, and a line that sounds
  translated will be heard as translated.
- Check what rights the music tool gives you on the output before putting it on
  anything public.
